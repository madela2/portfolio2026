About me

I'm Madeleine Lyseggen Aas, a web development student at NTNU with an interest in creating accessible, user-friendly, and thoughtful digital solutions.

Who I am

I'm currently studying web development and building my skills through both individual and team-based projects. I enjoy working with frontend development, but I also like understanding how the different parts of a web application work together.

Through my studies, I've worked with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, PHP, Python, databases, APIs, and full-stack project work. I like turning ideas into interfaces that feel clear, structured, and intuitive to use.

What I care about

Accessibility and usability are especially important to me. I want the things I build to work well for different kinds of users, including people who rely on keyboard navigation, screen readers, or other assistive technologies.

I also care about creating solutions that feel calm, understandable, and well-organised. For me, good design is not just about how something looks, but also about how it works and how it makes people feel when they use it.

How I work

I like to work in a structured and reflective way. I often spend time thinking through both the user experience and the implementation, and I care about making decisions that feel intentional.

In team projects, I value collaboration, clear communication, and being honest about what I contributed. I also enjoy learnign from others and improving as I go.

What I'm building towards

Right now, I'm continuing to strengthen my frontend and full-stack skills while building a portfolio that reflects both what I've learned and how I like to work.

My goal is to grom into a developer who creates accessible and meaningful digital experiences with both technical care and user needs in mind.

A little more about me

I enjoy learning by building real projects and seeing how ideas develop from early concepts to working solutions. I'm especially motivated by projects where I can combine code, design thinking, and practical problem-solving.

This portfolio is a way for me to show both what I've worked on and the kind of developer I'm becoming.
